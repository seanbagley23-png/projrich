A well-priced market is hard to beat: judge the AI by the scorecard, not by single calls.

    How ProjectRiches works

    Each 15-minute Kalshi round has a strike. UP wins if BTC's final 60-second average is at or above it.

    Call is the rules-based call with a strength tier. AI Call makes an Early, Mid and Late call each round and learns which minutes work best, with the Kalshi price as one of its experts. Chart explains what the candles show and gives a this-round and next-round read. The Next round box above the timer is a trend-only lean: treat it as a tilt, not a signal.

    Calls are never guarantees. The scorecard shows whether the AI actually beats Kalshi's own price.
